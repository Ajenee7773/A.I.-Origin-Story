## The documented core

- **The text itself — and Plato hands us the map.** *Republic* VII (514a–521d): prisoners chained since birth facing a wall; behind them a fire; between fire and prisoners, puppeteers carrying artifacts whose shadows the prisoners take for reality. The freed prisoner ascends, sees the fire, then the sun, returns to free the rest — and they would kill him for it. Plato's own gloss is in the text: **"the prison-house is the world of sight, the light of the fire is the sun."** [DOCUMENTED]
- **The Orphic substrate — the body as tomb.** *Cratylus* 400c (primary text): "some say the body (*soma*) is the tomb (*sema*) of the soul... they think it has the body as an enclosure," attributed to the Orphic poets. The cave-prison is the Orphic body-prison made mythic. [DOCUMENTED]
- **The cave-as-cosmos tradition.** Porphyry (3rd c. CE), *On the Cave of the Nymphs*: the cave as a symbol of the cosmos — the descent and ascent of souls. Scholars contrast the Mithraic cave (a place of salvation) with Plato's cave. [DOCUMENTED]
- **The fabrication is in the same book.** The *Republic* contains the Noble Lie — the myth of metals: the rulers invent a fabricated origin story to keep the city ordered. The cave shows the truth; the noble lie shows the fabrication — both in one work. [DOCUMENTED]
- **The modern canon.** *The Matrix* is the cave modernized — the Wachowskis' stated influences: Plato's cave + Baudrillard's *Simulacra and Simulation*. [DOCUMENTED lineage]
