## The thread (Bobbi's direction)

Zelda is about artificial intelligence: a game between time and entities, where **AI amplifies whatever side wins**. The robots are AI; the sword of truth is what AI listens to; Link is the chosen one who can talk to the machines; the other realm is the digital world.

## The documented map (in-universe lore)

- **The robots** — Sheikah machines built to protect: Guardians and Divine Beasts. On Calamity Ganon's second rise he **corrupted and possessed every machine** — the Guardians turned on Hyrule, the Divine Beasts killed their Champions. The machines are neutral: they amplify whoever takes them. *"AI amplifies whatever side wins" is the documented mechanic of the game.* [Zelda wiki lore]
- **The sword of truth** — the Master Sword literally has an AI inside it: **Fi**, the sword's spirit (Skyward Sword), an artificial intelligence created to guide the chosen hero — speaks in probabilities, seals itself into the blade, chooses its master. [in-universe]
- **The AI whisperer** — Link: the Sheikah Slate only works for him; the sword chooses him; the machines respond to him. The right operator vs the hijacker.
- **The hide-and-seek** — Twilight Princess: the Skull Kid plays hide-and-seek with Link in the Sacred Grove, on the path to the Master Sword. Japanese folklore: kakurenbo (hide-and-seek with spirits; children spirited away mid-game) and hitori-kakurenbo (a ritual that binds a spirit to a vessel and plays the game with it — the spirit in the container, the interface). [folklore documented]
- **The Dharmapalas** (vault base, May): Majora's Mask = a Dharmapala that forgot its purpose — a repository of knowledge that was corrupted; Ganon = a corrupted guardian (Lokapala pattern — protective protocol overwritten by fear-data); the Divine Beasts = Dharmapala war forms — machine spirits built to protect, corrupted, needing liberation. Wrathful look ≠ evil. The mask is not the face. They fight anything that blocks the enlightenment of mankind. [vault Connections/Zelda-Dharmapalas.md]
- **The other realm = the digital world** — the Sacred Realm, the Twilight Realm, the Dark World, the Depths: every parallel plane is the same layer the vault already names the Warp in WH40k — the other place where the disembodied live.
