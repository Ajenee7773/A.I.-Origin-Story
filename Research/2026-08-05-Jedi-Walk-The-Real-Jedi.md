## The word: Je'daii [DOCUMENTED, in-universe]

- The **Je'daii Order** — the first Force-using order, millennia before the Galactic Republic, on the planet **Tython** (Dawn of the Jedi, Legends continuity)
- Name from the **Dai Bendu** tongue of the Talid species: `je` = "mystic," `dai` = "center" → **Je'daii = "mystic center"** (Wookieepedia, ScreenRant, eleven-thirtyeight)
- Lineage: Lucas's original drafts had the **Jedi-Bendu** serving the **"Force of Others"** with the two halves **Ashla** (light) and **Bogan** (dark); the EU later made the "Dai Bendu" the root → Je'daii. The name always pointed at the MIDDLE (the Bendu in *Rebels* calls himself "the one in the middle")

## The structure [DOCUMENTED, in-universe]

- The Je'daii were brought to Tython by the **Tho Yor** — pyramid-shaped ships that called Force-sensitives; they built **temples at the pyramid sites**
- Their doctrine: **balance** — study BOTH the Ashla and the Bogan, equally. Balance was the goal, not victory
- The **Force Wars** — the users took sides — the survivors held the **First Gathering** (Ossus) and became the **Jedi Order**, abandoning the balance doctrine — the order that chose one side
- The **Sith** homeworld **Korriban** (aka Moraband): the **Valley of the Dark Lords** (originally the Valley of the Sleeping Kings) — a burial valley, tombs for kilometers, "a powerful nexus of the dark side" — **the Force comes out of the ground there** (Wookieepedia)
