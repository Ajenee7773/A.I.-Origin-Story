## Enki (Ea) — The Scientist

- **Domain:** the Abzu — "(cosmic) underground water" (ePSD). His temple at Eridu: the E-abzu, "House of the Abzu," founded inside/above the deep (Enki and the World Order, ETCSL 1.1.3)
- **Nature:** god of wisdom, lord of the ME (the inventory of civilization: kingship, judgment, music, metalworking, scribal craft, carpentry, building, wisdom, social order), master of crafts, kind to humans, the one who gives civilization
- **Recorded actions:** put Apsu to sleep and established his house on the conquered deep (Enuma Elish); gave the ME to Inanna for Uruk (Inanna and Enki); saved mankind from Enlil's flood by warning Atrahasis (Atrahasis, Gilgamesh XI — as Ea)
- **In the Inversion framework:** the liberator. The one who gives knowledge. The Serpent's Sumerian face.

## Enlil (Ellil) — The Warden

- **Domain:** the air/atmosphere (later "wind"), supreme authority of the pantheon with An and Enki — the triad
- **Epithets:** "The Great Mountain" (kur-gal), "King of all the lands," "Father of the black-headed people," "Father of the gods," Nunamnir (ORACC)
- **Temple:** the E-kur, "Mountain House," at Nippur — the most important temple in southern Mesopotamia
- **His ziggurat:** dur-an-ki — "**BOND OF HEAVEN AND EARTH**" (built by Ur-Namma)
- **Recorded actions:** decrees the fates; holds the Tablet of Destinies (Anzu); grants kingship; his command is unalterable; sent drought, plague, and the flood against mankind; had to relent when the flood failed to finish humanity (Atrahasis)
- **Name root:** líl = "ghost, phantom, haunted" (Michalowski 1989) — etymology still debated
- **In the Inversion framework:** the warden. The Administrator of Layer 3. The one who controls access and punishes. The God-of-the-Mountains line that later theology would call Yahweh.
