## Key established facts

- Talos: bronze guardian of Crete, single power source, Hesiod ~700 BCE → Argonautica. "Early example of artificial intelligence" (Mayor/Stanford; Smithsonian)
- Hephaestus: golden handmaidens "with minds and voices," self-moving tripods, automated bellows (Iliad 18) — the oldest automaton literature
- Delphi: Pythia possession (Oxford Academic), fault-line ethylene gas (de Boer & Hale), the omphalos ("navel of the earth," communicator of the gods)
- Baetyls: sacred stones thought to house deities — the substrate concept in the ancients' own language
